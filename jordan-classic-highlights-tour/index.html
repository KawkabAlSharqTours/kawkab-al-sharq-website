Classic Highlights

Jordan Classic Highlights

Not every guest wants an alternative route — many simply want the sites Jordan is famous for, covered efficiently and comfortably in a short trip. This is that itinerary: the same core sites as most competitors, run on a tight, realistic schedule with well-located hotels. It's intentionally the easiest itinerary in our portfolio to book for a first-time visitor.

  • Duration5 Days / 4 Nights
  • Best ForFirst-time visitors, short leave allowance
  • StylePrivate or shared-group tour
  • Starts / EndsAmman
  • Group Size6–15 travelers

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 01Day

    Arrival — Amman City Tour

    Arrival: Meet and greet at Queen Alia International Airport, transfer to hotel.

    Afternoon: Amman Citadel, the Roman Theatre, and Rainbow Street for shopping and dinner.

    Overnight: Amman, 4–5★ hotel

  2. 02Day

    Jerash — Ajloun — Dead Sea

    Morning: Jerash, one of the best-preserved Roman provincial cities in the world (Oval Plaza, Cardo, Hadrian's Arch).

    Afternoon: Ajloun Castle, then transfer to the Dead Sea (approx. 1.5 hrs).

    Evening: Free time to float in the Dead Sea and use resort spa facilities.

    Overnight: Dead Sea, 4–5★ resort

  3. 03Day

    Dead Sea — Madaba — Mount Nebo — Petra

    Morning: Free time at the resort, then Madaba's Church of St. George (the Madaba Map mosaic) and Mount Nebo, traditionally where Moses viewed the Promised Land.

    Afternoon: Scenic drive to Petra via the Desert Highway (approx. 3 hrs).

    Overnight: Wadi Musa (Petra), 4–5★ hotel

  4. 04Day

    Petra Full Day

    Full day: The Siq, the Treasury, the Street of Facades, the Royal Tombs, and — for guests who want it — the 800-step climb to the Monastery. Guided in the morning, free time in the afternoon.

    Overnight: Wadi Musa (Petra), 4–5★ hotel

  5. 05Day

    Wadi Rum — Departure

    Morning: Transfer to Wadi Rum for a classic 2-hour jeep tour of the main viewpoints (Lawrence's Spring, the Red Dune, Khazali Canyon).

    Afternoon: Transfer to Aqaba or Amman for departure.
