Hidden Civilizations

Standard "historical Jordan" tours mean Jerash, Petra, and Mount Nebo. This route keeps Petra but reframes the rest of the trip around Jordan's lesser-known layers: Decapolis cities competitors skip (Umm Qais, Pella), the desert hunting-lodge frescoes of the Umayyad caliphs, a black basalt Byzantine ghost town, a Hellenistic palace carved for a single noble family, and Crusader-era castles reached by the King's Highway rather than the motorway.

  • Duration7 Days / 6 Nights
  • Best ForHistory & archaeology enthusiasts
  • StylePrivate / small-group tour
  • Starts / EndsAmman
  • Group Size6–10 travelers

Day-by-Day Itinerary

  1. 01Day

    Amman — Iraq al-Amir — Amman Citadel

    Morning: Iraq al-Amir: Qasr al-Abd, a 2nd-century BCE Hellenistic palace carved for the Tobiad noble family, set in a quiet valley west of Amman that most itineraries never reach.

    Afternoon: Amman Citadel (Jabal al-Qal'a) and the Roman Theatre, then the Jordan Museum for context before the trip heads into the field.

    Overnight: Amman, 4★ hotel

  2. 02Day

    Eastern Desert Castles Loop

    Full day: A private loop most agencies do not run at all: Qasr Kharana's mysterious fortified halls, Qusayr Amra (a UNESCO World Heritage Umayyad bathhouse with startlingly intact 8th-century frescoes, including an early zodiac ceiling), and Qasr Azraq, T.E. Lawrence's WWI winter headquarters, finishing at the Azraq Wetland Reserve.

    Overnight: Amman, 4★ hotel

  3. 03Day

    Umm el-Jimal — Umm Qais — Pella

    Morning: Umm el-Jimal, the "Black Gem of the Desert" — an entire Byzantine and early Islamic town built from black basalt, remarkably intact and virtually unvisited.

    Afternoon: Umm Qais (ancient Gadara), a Decapolis city with Roman theatres and colonnades looking out over the Sea of Galilee, the Golan Heights, and the Yarmouk Valley — one of Jordan's best views, rarely on group itineraries.

    Late afternoon: Pella, an archaeological site with continuous occupation from the Neolithic through Islamic periods, excavated but lightly visited.

    Overnight: Ajloun or northern Jordan Valley lodge

  4. 04Day

    Ajloun Castle — Jerash — As-Salt

    Morning: Ajloun Castle, then Jerash — included because it earns its place, but visited via the lesser-used North Theatre and Temple of Artemis approach rather than the main tour-bus entrance, with a private guide able to linger past group departure times.

    Afternoon: As-Salt's Ottoman-era old town and its 1920s trading-family mansions.

    Overnight: Amman, 4★ hotel

  5. 05Day

    King's Highway: Karak — Shobak

    Full day: Drive the King's Highway rather than the Desert Highway, stopping at Karak Castle (the Crusader stronghold of Raynald of Châtillon) and the more remote, dramatically sited Shobak Castle, Crusader Jordan's first fortress — usually a rushed 20-minute photo stop elsewhere, here given proper time.

    Overnight: Petra region, 4★ hotel

  6. 06Day

    Little Petra, Beidha & Petra (Back-Door Entry)

    Morning: Beidha Neolithic village and Little Petra (Siq al-Barid), then the shepherd's trail into Petra from the north.

    Afternoon: Petra's Royal Tombs, the Treasury, and — with the extra time gained by skipping the main Siq queue — the Basin restaurant area and Petra Museum, which most day-tour schedules cut.

    Overnight: Petra region, 4★ hotel

  7. 07Day

    Petra Monastery Trail — Departure

    Morning: Wadi Farasa and the Monastery (Ad-Deir) via the 800-step trail, then transfer to Amman or Aqaba for departure.
