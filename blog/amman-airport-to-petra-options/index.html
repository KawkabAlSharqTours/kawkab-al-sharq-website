How to Get From Amman Airport to Petra (All Your Options Compared)

Practical Guide

Queen Alia International Airport to Petra is roughly 3 hours by road via the Desert Highway, or longer via the more scenic King's Highway. Here's how the main options compare.

OptionTimeBest For
Private transfer~3 hrs (Desert Highway)Direct, flexible timing, no transfers
Shared shuttle/JETT coach~4 hrs incl. stopsBudget travelers comfortable with a fixed schedule
Rental car (self-drive)~3 hrsIndependent travelers wanting full route flexibility
Multi-day route with stops2–3 daysFirst-time visitors who want Amman, Dead Sea, and Madaba en route rather than a direct transfer

Why We Usually Recommend Against a Direct Transfer

Going straight from the airport to Petra skips Amman, the Dead Sea, and Madaba entirely — reasonable if Petra is truly all you have time for, but most travelers regret not seeing Jordan's other highlights on the way. Our Classic Highlights itinerary spreads this same distance across 3 days with stops that make the drive itself worthwhile.

Desert Highway vs King's Highway

The Desert Highway is faster; the King's Highway is more scenic and passes Karak and Shobak castles, at the cost of extra time. See our full comparison: Desert Highway vs King's Highway.

What We Include

Every one of our itineraries includes private vehicle transport with a driver — no separate transfer booking needed, and no shared-shuttle schedule to work around.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I fly directly into a closer airport?
King Hussein International Airport in Aqaba is closer to Wadi Rum and the south, and works well as a departure point after Petra and Wadi Rum on itineraries that end in the south.

Is self-driving in Jordan straightforward for tourists?
It's possible, but most of our clients prefer a private driver — road conditions and driving norms outside major highways can be unfamiliar, and a driver frees you to actually look at the scenery.
