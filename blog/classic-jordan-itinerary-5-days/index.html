The Classic Jordan Itinerary: Petra, Wadi Rum & the Dead Sea in 5 Days

Itinerary Guide

Not every traveler wants an alternative route — many simply want the sites Jordan is famous for, covered efficiently and comfortably. Jordan Classic Highlights is that itinerary: the same core sites as most competitors, run on a tight, realistic schedule with well-located hotels.

Day-by-Day Snapshot

  • Day 1: Arrival, Amman Citadel, Roman Theatre, Rainbow Street
  • Day 2: Jerash's Roman ruins, Ajloun Castle, transfer to the Dead Sea
  • Day 3: Madaba's mosaics, Mount Nebo, drive to Petra
  • Day 4: Full day in Petra — the Siq, the Treasury, the Royal Tombs, optionally the Monastery
  • Day 5: Wadi Rum jeep tour, departure

Why This Is Our Easiest Itinerary to Book

It's intentionally the most straightforward trip in our portfolio — well-known sites, efficient sequencing, and no specialized fitness or interest required. If it's your first trip to Jordan and you're not sure which themed route to pick, this is the safe, reliable choice.

What It Doesn't Include

This route doesn't include Petra's back-door Wadi Muthlim entrance, deep desert trekking, or homestay experiences — those live in our Adventure, Historical, and Cultural Immersion itineraries. Classic Highlights prioritizes efficiency and the essentials over depth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can this be extended?
Yes — it also runs as a 3-day Petra and Wadi Rum stopover, or can be extended to 10–14 days combined with another themed itinerary.

What does it cost?
From 465 JD (≈ $655) per person, twin-share, Standard tier — full pricing and accommodation details are on the itinerary page.
