The Ultimate Jordan Wellness Trip: Hot Springs, Ecolodges & Desert Silence

Itinerary Guide

Typical "relaxation" add-ons in Jordan mean one afternoon at a Dead Sea resort bolted onto a sightseeing tour. Our Desert Silence & Healing itinerary is built the other way around: slow travel through Jordan's three genuinely restorative landscapes, with the Red Sea as a closing stretch of pure downtime.

Hammamat Ma'in

A valley of mineral-rich hot-spring waterfalls cascading from 60°C thermal sources — an alternative to the Dead Sea that most itineraries skip entirely, and a genuinely different sensation from a still-water float.

Feynan Ecolodge

Solar-powered and candlelit after dark, with no phone signal or road noise, inside the Dana Biosphere Reserve. Evenings here include a rooftop stargazing session and a traditional Bedouin tea ceremony, with all meals plant-forward and locally sourced.

A Private, Low-Density Wadi Rum Camp

Rather than a shared jeep-tour camp, this itinerary places you at a small camp positioned away from generator-run sites — chosen specifically for night silence and dark skies, not spectacle.

The Dead Sea, Done Properly

A full afternoon (not a rushed 90-minute stop) for floating, mineral mud treatment, and a private cabana, plus a spa treatment included at the resort.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this itinerary physically demanding?
No — unlike our Adventure route, this is intentionally low-exertion. Optional light nature walks are available but nothing is required.

What's the best season for this trip?
Winter is arguably ideal — Ma'in's hot springs and the Dead Sea's warm water are especially welcome against cool air, and Feynan's candlelit evenings feel more atmospheric in winter's longer nights.
