Jordan for Corporate Retreats: Why the Dead Sea and Petra Work as Offsite Venues

Group Travel

A strong corporate retreat destination needs two things most places don't combine well: meeting-ready venues and experiences that genuinely work as group activities rather than sightseeing tacked onto a conference. Jordan does both.

Meeting Infrastructure Where You Need It

Meeting rooms and AV equipment are arranged at Amman and Dead Sea resort properties for daytime strategy sessions — you're not sacrificing productivity for the destination.

A Sample 4-Day / 3-Night Retreat

  • Day 1: Amman arrival, welcome reception and dinner with private event space
  • Day 2: Dead Sea — morning strategy session in a private meeting room, afternoon spa and Dead Sea floating, evening group activity (cooking competition or mixology class)
  • Day 3: Full-day guided Petra visit, closing with a private group dinner arranged inside or near the Siq (subject to permits and group size)
  • Day 4: Wadi Rum team-building — a 4x4 navigation challenge and obstacle activities in small competing groups, run by Bedouin drivers as team guides

Why the Activities Work as Team-Building, Not Just Sightseeing

The Wadi Rum navigation challenge and small-group competition format genuinely function as team exercises, not a break from work — and a private Petra evening event is a strong, memorable closing moment for an incentive trip specifically.

Custom Branding and CSR Add-Ons

Custom branding (welcome signage, gift bags, a branded desert dinner setup) is available on request, along with an optional CSR add-on — a half-day visit to a Bedouin women's cooperative or RSCN community project in Dana, positioned as genuine engagement rather than a photo opportunity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a corporate retreat cost?
From 780 JD (≈ $1,100) per person for the Essential tier (4★ properties, standard meeting room), up to 1,050 JD (≈ $1,480) for Premium (5★ properties, branded event setup, Petra Siq-area private dinner). Fully custom Ultra-tier events are quoted on request.

Is there a dedicated event coordinator?
Yes — for groups of 15+, a dedicated on-ground event coordinator works alongside your internal organizer throughout the trip.
