Sleeping in the Dana Biosphere Reserve: A Guide to Dana Guesthouse

Differentiator Guide

Dana village sits on the rim of Jordan's largest nature reserve, and Dana Guesthouse's cliff-edge rooms look straight out over it. Most Jordan itineraries treat Dana as a driving stop on the way to Petra; ours treats it as a destination worth a night on its own.

What the Reserve Actually Is

The Dana Biosphere Reserve spans multiple biogeographical zones within a single protected area — from Mediterranean woodland at the rim down through desert canyon terrain toward Feynan. It's Jordan's largest nature reserve and one of its most ecologically significant.

An Evening at Dana Guesthouse

Rooms open onto the reserve at golden hour — a genuinely different pace from a city hotel or resort. On our Living Jordan itinerary, the evening includes tea with a local family discussing reserve life and Bedouin/village relations; on other routes, it's simply unstructured time on the terrace.

What You Can Do From Dana

A full-day guided trek down to Feynan (roughly 14km, moderate-to-challenging) is the signature route on our Adventure itinerary. For a gentler pace, our Wellness itinerary offers a short, easy nature walk with an RSCN ranger instead — no strenuous trekking required.

Where This Fits

Dana Guesthouse appears on our Rift Valley Traverse, Desert Silence & Healing, and Living Jordan itineraries — each uses the same base but a different pace and activity level.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be an experienced hiker to stay at Dana?
No — only our Adventure itinerary requires the full Dana-to-Feynan trek. Our Wellness and Cultural Immersion routes offer gentler ways to experience the reserve.

Is Dana worth visiting if I'm short on time?
If you have 6+ days, yes — it pairs naturally with Feynan and Petra on the way south, adding a genuinely different landscape to the trip without a major detour.

Want to Stay at Dana?

Included on our Rift Valley Traverse, Desert Silence & Healing, and Living Jordan itineraries.
