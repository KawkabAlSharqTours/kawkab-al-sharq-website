Best Jordan Hotels With Pools for Families

Family Travel

On our Jordan for Families itinerary, we deliberately choose hotels with pools and family/connecting rooms over properties selected purely for character — a pool at the end of a hot day of sightseeing matters more to most kids than a boutique lobby.

Amman: Family Room or Connecting Rooms, Pool Access

Amman hotels on our family route are chosen for connecting or family room configurations and pool access, positioned for easy access to the Citadel and Rainbow Street without a long transfer after a travel day.

The Dead Sea: Water Park Over Adults-Oriented Spa Resort

This is the single biggest hotel decision on the whole itinerary. Standard Dead Sea resorts lean toward adult spa culture; we specifically book resorts with a water park or multiple pools alongside beach access, since floating alone rarely holds a child's attention for a full day.

Petra: Pool Access After a Long Walking Day

Petra days involve significant walking regardless of pacing — a hotel pool to unwind in afterward makes a genuine difference to how the next day starts, especially with younger children.

Wadi Rum: Proper Facilities, Not Just Character

Our family camp choice prioritizes proper toilets and showers over the more rustic, character-driven camps we use on other itineraries — a practical trade that matters more with children than with adult travelers.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do all your family itinerary hotels have pools?
Amman, Dead Sea, and Petra nights all include pool access; Wadi Rum's family camp prioritizes proper facilities over a pool, which isn't available in the desert regardless of camp choice.

Can hotel choices be upgraded?
Yes — our Comfort tier upgrades to a 5★ Dead Sea water park resort and upgraded Petra/Wadi Rum accommodation. See the full itinerary for pricing.
