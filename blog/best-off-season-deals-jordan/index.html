Best Off-Season Deals: When to Book Jordan for the Lowest Prices

Seasonal Guide

Every itinerary in our portfolio runs at a discounted rate during Jordan's two off-peak windows — 20% off in Winter Value season, 15% off in Summer Heat season — with activities and pacing adjusted for weather, not just discounted and left unchanged.

Winter Value Season: December 1 – February 28

The deeper of the two discounts, and arguably the better trade-off — see our guide to visiting Jordan in winter for what to actually expect. Excludes the December 20–January 5 holiday peak, which reverts to standard rates.

Summer Heat Season: June 15 – August 31

A smaller discount, reflecting that the Dead Sea and Aqaba stay busier with regional weekend guests even in peak heat. See our guide to visiting Jordan in summer for how we adjust scheduling around the temperatures.

Blackout Dates to Know

Standard high-season rates apply during December 20–January 5, Easter week, and Eid al-Fitr/Eid al-Adha (dates shift yearly) regardless of month, since these are peak-demand windows even inside an otherwise quiet season.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Comfort and Premium tiers also get the off-season discount?
Yes — Comfort/Premium tiers and single supplements discount at the same percentage as the Standard tier shown above.

Which off-season window has better weather for sightseeing?
Winter is generally more comfortable for walking-heavy days (Petra, Historical route), while summer's water-based stops (Dead Sea, Aqaba) hold up well regardless of heat.
