Our Story

Kawkab Al-Sharq Tourism & Travel was founded in 2024 by Munir Abu Aisha, building on more than 25 years of professional experience in tourism and travel, as a Jordan-based inbound tour operator serving travelers from Europe, the Americas, and Asia.

Our vision: to become a trusted and distinguished tourism company specializing in inbound tourism to Jordan, and a preferred partner for international travelers, tour operators, and travel agencies.

Our mission: to provide professional, reliable, and high-quality tourism services that let visitors discover Jordan comfortably, safely, and memorably — drawing on extensive industry experience to support travelers from trip planning through the completion of their journey.
