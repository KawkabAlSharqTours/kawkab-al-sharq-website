Who We Are

Local Expertise, Personal Attention

Kawkab Al-Sharq Tourism & Travel was founded in 2024 by Munir Abu Aisha, building on more than 25 years of professional experience in tourism and travel, to give travelers from Europe, the Americas, and Asia an authentic, well-organized way to experience Jordan.

As a fully inbound operator based in Jordan, every itinerary is planned, guided, and supported on the ground, from airport pickup to your final departure — with the goal of becoming a trusted, preferred partner for international travelers, tour operators, and travel agencies alike.
